Marura Gardens is more than a wedding venue — it's a year-round lifestyle and events destination. From classic car Sundays to family camping weekends, we blend natural beauty with curated experiences.

Premium Events Venue

Wooden cabins, safari glamping tents, campfire areas and nature trails for a perfect escape.

Monthly gatherings for collectors and enthusiasts with exhibitions, live music and food markets.

  • Classic car exhibitions
  • Vintage motorcycle displays
  • Awards & live music
  • Auto vendors & photography

Pet Events

  • Pet fashion & grooming
  • Veterinary clinics
  • Adoption & agility courses

Weekend Family Park

  • Children's playground
  • Horse riding & archery
  • Food festival weekends
  • Farmers' market

Corporate Packages

Tailored retreats, team building and outdoor workshops designed to inspire and connect.

  • Leadership retreats
  • Executive meetings
  • Annual staff parties
